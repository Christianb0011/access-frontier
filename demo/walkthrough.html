Access requests dominate IT work

58.6%

of 1,960 real English IT-support tickets match access, account or mailbox keywords.

Each one is a security decision

Grant the wrong one and it's an incident. Resolve it slowly and an employee can't work. Enterprises want agents to do this with real tool calls.

Evals grade the reply

Most benchmarks check the model's final answer, not what it actually executed along the way. That's where the risk lives.
